Identity

Aesthetic

Soft glassmorphism on a muted lavender base. Translucent cards, subtle borders, and gentle shadows create depth without noise.

Personality

Thoughtful and technical, yet warm and approachable. Confident but not loud — the design steps back and lets the content speak.

Motion

Spring-based hover lifts feel alive. Transitions are fast (200–400 ms) and purposeful — every animation has a reason.

Shape

Generously rounded corners (12–20 px). Pill-shaped labels and chips. No sharp edges — everything feels friendly and considered.

Colour philosophy

A purple-grey base anchors the UI. Saturated category colours provide accent and meaning — each domain has its own distinct hue.

Typography

IBM Plex Sans exclusively. Technical and humanist — the same font IBM uses in its developer tools. Weights 300–700 in active use.

Motion

Spring hover

cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)

Used on home windows, filter buttons, skill chips. Overshoots slightly before settling — feels alive and playful.

Standard ease

ease · 200–300 ms

Used on tabs, card hover lift, timeline dot scale. Smooth and neutral — for interactions that shouldn't feel bouncy.

Slide entrance

slideIn · opacity 0→1, translateY 16px→0

Applied to timeline items and showcase cards when their panel is shown. Staggered by 80 ms per item.

Slideshow crossfade

opacity · 600 ms ease

Home window slides fade between one another. Auto-advances every 4 s, pauses on hover.

Hover lift — cards

translateY(-3px) · 250 ms ease

Timeline cards and showcase cards lift gently on hover, accompanied by a soft box-shadow increase.

Hover lift — windows

translateY(-10px) rotate(-0.8deg) · 350 ms spring

Home windows lift more dramatically with a slight tilt — the most expressive interaction on the site.

Status pulse

statusPulse · 2.2 s ease-in-out infinite

The green dot next to "Open to opportunities" pulses gently — opacity and scale cycle to suggest presence.

Dot pill expand

width 6px→20px · 300 ms spring

Active slideshow dots expand into a pill shape. The spring curve makes the snap feel snappy but organic.

Guiding principle: Every animation should have a reason. Hover feedback, entrance transitions, and the living slideshow are all purposeful. Avoid decoration for its own sake — motion should communicate state or reward interaction.
